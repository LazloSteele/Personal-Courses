# Wine Knowledge Resources

## Knowledge

- [Official: WSET Level 2 Award in Wines (course page)](https://www.wsetglobal.com/qualifications/wset-level-2-award-in-wines/)
  The awarding body. Use for: exam format, the 8 principal + 22 regionally important varieties, what
  the syllabus covers. Overrides every other source on exam facts.
- [Official: WSET School London, Level 2 course page](https://wsetglobal.com/wset-school-london/wset-courses/wset-level-2-award-in-wines)
  Confirms 50 MCQ / 60 min, grade bands (Pass 55–69%, Merit 70–84%, Distinction 85%+) and 28 hours
  minimum study (16 guided, 11 personal, 1 exam). Use for: study planning.
- [Official: WSET Level 2 Systematic Approach to Tasting Wine (2023, Issue 2), PDF](https://www.WSETglobal.com/media/13156/wset_l2wines_sat_en_may2023_issue2.pdf)
  The tasting vocabulary the exam uses. Use for: every scale word in lessons (sweetness, acidity,
  tannin, alcohol, body, flavour intensity, quality).
- [Official: WSET Level 2 Wines session plans (2023, Issue 2), PDF](https://www.wsetglobal.com/media/13022/wset_l2wines_sessionplans_en_may2023_issue2.pdf)
  Educator plans, including suggested calibration wines (e.g. Beaujolais Villages vs Barolo for
  tannin/body). Use for: choosing bottles for tasting exercises.
- [Official: WSET SAT explainer](https://wset.co.uk/knowledge-centre/wset-systematic-approach-to-tasting-sat)
  What the SAT is and how it's structured (appearance → nose → palate → conclusions).
- Book: _Wines: Looking Behind the Label_ (WSET Level 2 textbook, issued with course enrolment)
  The examined content. Use for: anything the exam can ask. Not freely available online.
- [Wine Folly: At-home palate training exercise](https://winefolly.com/tutorial/diy-palate-training-wine-tasting/)
  Tea / lemon / sugar / vodka in red wine to isolate structure. Use for: home calibration lessons.
- [Wine Folly: What is residual sugar?](https://winefolly.com/deep-dive/what-is-residual-sugar-in-wine)
  Plain-language explainer. Use for: sweetness, fermentation basics.
- [Decanter: Tannins, what are they?](https://www.decanter.com/learn/tannins-45814/)
  Use for: tannin sources (skins, seeds, stems, oak) and feel.
- [Cornell (eCommons) enology document on wine mouthfeel](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/f0a13130-98a3-4ce7-a922-8e1f942481c4/content)
  Research-grade backing for body (alcohol, sugar, polysaccharides) and alcohol heat above ~14%.
- [Jancis Robinson: Wine courses guide + Purple Pages](https://www.jancisrobinson.com/learn/wine-courses)
  Purple Pages membership includes the online _Oxford Companion to Wine_. Use for: authoritative
  lookups once regions and varieties start.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Local WSET Approved Programme Provider (find via wsetglobal.com)
  Use for: the actual course, the exam, and structured group tastings with an educator. Business
  goal makes a classroom course worth considering, because the room is full of trade contacts.
- [GuildSomm](https://www.guildsomm.com/)
  Nonprofit, membership-based (paid; free podcasts). Study guides, quizzes, member forums with
  working sommeliers and buyers. Use for: trade-level questions once past Level 1.
- Study / tasting group (to set up)
  Splitting bottles among 3–5 people solves the "occasional tasting" constraint. Use for:
  comparative tastings and calibrating scale words against other palates.
