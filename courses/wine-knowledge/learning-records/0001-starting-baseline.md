# Starting baseline: beginner on the WSET path, for consulting work

At intake the learner said they're a beginner with no wine certification. They chose WSET over the
Court of Master Sommeliers, and business/consulting as the reason. That means they need no service
practical, and the Level 2 exam is theory-only multiple choice built on SAT vocabulary.

**Evidence**: Intake answers, 2026-10-12.

**Implications**: Start from structure and fermentation. Teach no regions before the five
structural dials are solid. Frame examples around client-facing use (list building, staff briefings,
pairing). Tasting is occasional, so one bottle has to cover a whole lesson.
