Tell them apart

  • Acidity or tannin? Swallow and wait. Watering means acidity; drying means tannin.
  • Sweet or fruity? Sweetness is sugar on the tongue. Fruit is a flavour you smell. A dry wine can be very fruity.
  • Sugar and alcohol trade off. Yeast turns grape sugar into alcohol, so sugar left in the wine is sugar that didn't become alcohol.
