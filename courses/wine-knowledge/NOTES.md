# Notes

## Learner profile (from intake, 2026-10-12)
- Target: WSET path. Level 1 Award in Wines, then Level 2 Award in Wines. Not CMS.
- Motivation: business / consulting (F&B). Frame lessons around client-facing use: describing
  wines to clients and staff, building lists by style, pairing for a menu.
- Baseline: beginner.
- Tasting access: occasional. Design each tasting so one inexpensive bottle does a whole lesson.
  Home calibration (tea, lemon, sugar) stretches it further.

## Teaching decisions
- Use WSET L2 SAT 2023 (Issue 2) vocabulary everywhere. The exam is multiple choice on that
  vocabulary, so precise scale words matter.
- Quizzes mimic the exam: four options, one correct, equal-length answers.
- Spacing plan: open each session with 3–5 retrieval questions from earlier lessons before new
  material.

## Lesson roadmap (draft, revise as records accrue)
1. Structure: the five dials (sweetness, acidity, tannin, alcohol, body). DONE: lesson 0001
2. How wine is made: fermentation, red vs white vs rosé, oak, and how each choice shows up in the dials
3. Climate and ripeness: cool vs warm climate (acidity, alcohol, body, fruit character)
4. Principal whites: Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, Pinot Grigio/Gris
5. Principal reds: Pinot Noir, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah/Shiraz
6. Food and wine pairing principles (WSET approach)
7. Labels and quality terms; sparkling and sweet/fortified styles
8. Regionally important varieties (the 22)
